## $ whoami

Software engineer with a taste for minimal, keyboard-driven tools. I build things that stay out of your way — terminal UIs, Neovim configs, Tmux workflows, fzf filters that make you faster without getting in your face.

I write about the terminal, the tools I use, and the things I build. This site is itself a showcase: Tokyo Night themed, keyboard-navigable, dark/light toggle, built with Next.js and MDX.
